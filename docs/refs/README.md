## station-mocks/

Our own hand-drawn station mocks (not third-party), kept as the reference for station composition; the code that
draws them is `tools/shipgen/mocks/` on the branch `stationgen/architecture-integration`. `sheet.png` shows all four
beside the old wheel. Each has a department view and a labelled view (cyan: a room's largest rectangle).

- mining: "ore spine". A rectangular processing spine straight through a ring and out past the hull at both ends,
  docks at the tips, the machinery mid-spine, the civil half and a court north of it, voids south. The user: "pretty cool".
- tradingB: "faceted bazaar". A 16-sided hull so every divider and wing lands on a flat face. The user: faceted is good.
- tradingA: "harbour wheel". Its warehouse with an integrated dock was liked.
- farming: "greenhouse hub". A ring round a square hub with open space between.
- Throughout: arms are inhabited wings, never a room on a stick.
