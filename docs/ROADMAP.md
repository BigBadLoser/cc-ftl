# Roadmap

Planned feature work, in rough order. No dates: the order changes as the game takes shape. Shipped work moves to the [changelog](../CHANGELOG.md).

## Current goals

These aren't features. They're the aims we use to choose which features to build next.

- **Physical encounters.** Encounters happen with real ships, crew and salvage you can see and act on, not just text outcomes. First, the stubbed outcomes get implemented, then several new encounters are added.
- **Discovery and rewards.** Exploring turns up valuable equipment, unusual resources and useful information.
- **World opportunities.** Better points of interest, more variation between regions, and better information about what's nearby.

## Now

- **Away sites and salvage, rebuilt.** Crew work off the ship on wrecks, stage loot at the shuttle, and haul several piles per trip. Crew get air and suit needs like any other drive. ([#286](https://github.com/BigBadLoser/cc-ftl/issues/286), PR [#297](https://github.com/BigBadLoser/cc-ftl/pull/297); also [#268](https://github.com/BigBadLoser/cc-ftl/issues/268))
- **Rest retune.** Crew stay awake about 20 minutes and sleep about 7 in a bed, so a long shift no longer ends after a minute or two. ([#278](https://github.com/BigBadLoser/cc-ftl/issues/278))
- **Boarding parties and shipyard contractors on away sites.** Boarders and yard contractors travel and work off-ship the same way salvage crews do. ([#287](https://github.com/BigBadLoser/cc-ftl/issues/287))

## Next

- **Mental breaks.** Crew with unmet needs or bad stretches can snap: refuse work, wander, brawl or quit, with alerts and recovery. Low mood finally has consequences. ([#175](https://github.com/BigBadLoser/cc-ftl/issues/175), [#51](https://github.com/BigBadLoser/cc-ftl/issues/51))
- **Crew relationships.** Crew form bonds and grudges, which feed into mood, breaks and the new traits. ([#239](https://github.com/BigBadLoser/cc-ftl/issues/239))

## Later: major features

Not in order, except that the two weapon items come first.

- **Ship weapon upgrades.** Slot upgrades into ship weapons.
- **More ship weapon types.** Flak and other new weapon families.
- **Drones.**
- **Robots you research and build.** Research robot designs and build your own robots.
- **Construction pods.** Build pods that do hull work away from the ship.
- **New species.**

## Later: smaller features

Not in order.

- **Events in the world.** Story events happen outside transit and leave something physical behind: a derelict to explore, boarders to fight. ([#173](https://github.com/BigBadLoser/cc-ftl/issues/173), [#60](https://github.com/BigBadLoser/cc-ftl/issues/60))
- **Prisoners and prizes.** At the end of a fight, take prisoners or let them go. Hold them in a brig, ransom or recruit them, and sell or keep captured hulls. ([#116](https://github.com/BigBadLoser/cc-ftl/issues/116))
- **Contraband inspections in the world.** Navy shuttles dock, inspectors search your hold on screen, and you can bribe, refuse or fight. ([#138](https://github.com/BigBadLoser/cc-ftl/issues/138))
- **A second income and things to spend on.** Another way to earn besides deliveries, and real costs to spend credits on. Needs your design call. ([#174](https://github.com/BigBadLoser/cc-ftl/issues/174))
- **Weapons and gear to chase.** Ten crew weapons with art become items you can find, buy and equip. ([#49](https://github.com/BigBadLoser/cc-ftl/issues/49))
- **Goals after the tutorial.** A rolling captain's log of goals so there is something to work toward past act 3, with a pull toward deeper space. ([#172](https://github.com/BigBadLoser/cc-ftl/issues/172), [#52](https://github.com/BigBadLoser/cc-ftl/issues/52))
- **Less dead waiting.** Start sectors with more to find, and fewer idle hops. ([#171](https://github.com/BigBadLoser/cc-ftl/issues/171))
- **Food and cooking.** The galley posts cooking jobs, hydroponics gives real crops, and meals are made from ingredients. ([#59](https://github.com/BigBadLoser/cc-ftl/issues/59))
- **Vehicles built at their bays.** Pods and shuttles are built at their own bay, not at the fabricator. ([#48](https://github.com/BigBadLoser/cc-ftl/issues/48))
- **More job types.** Escort runs, high-risk prisoners and classified cargo. ([#179](https://github.com/BigBadLoser/cc-ftl/issues/179))
- **Clearer ship overview and alerts.** A labelled by-the-numbers overview, and clickable alerts for low fuel, food and money. ([#164](https://github.com/BigBadLoser/cc-ftl/issues/164))
- **Small quality-of-life fixes.** Research hints on locked items, clearer trade wording, fuel on job cards, a skills row in the crew inspector. ([#176](https://github.com/BigBadLoser/cc-ftl/issues/176))
- **UI scale setting.** Scale the whole interface from the display settings. ([#215](https://github.com/BigBadLoser/cc-ftl/issues/215))

## Parked

- **Addictions.** Drinker and stim-hooked crew, until there are things to get addicted to. ([#239](https://github.com/BigBadLoser/cc-ftl/issues/239))
