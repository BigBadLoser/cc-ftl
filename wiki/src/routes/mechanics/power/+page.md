# Power and reactors

Modules can produce or consume electrical power. A **positive** power value on a module definition represents generation; a **negative** value represents consumption.

- **Reactor:** generates power but produces heat.
- **Life support:** consumes power to generate breathable air and remove CO2.
- **Other equipment:** has its own consumption and priority; the available power budget constrains ship designs.

The [module reference](../../reference/modules/) lists the exact power values, construction costs, and sizes taken from CC-FTL's definitions.
