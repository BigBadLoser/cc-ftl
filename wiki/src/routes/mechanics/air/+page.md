Every room holds its own air, and air moves through open doors until the rooms match. A shut door stops it, which is what
you want when one side is on fire or open to space, and a nuisance when you only want two rooms to share air.

## Door vents

Every door has a **vent**. Select a door and press **Open vent** in its panel (press **Close vent** to seal it again).
With the vent open, air and pressure pass between the two sides even while the door is shut, slower than through an open
door. Fire, heat and smoke do not travel through it.

A vent looks after itself:

- It **shuts on its own** whenever either side has a hazard: fire, smoke, vacuum or thin air, bad air, or dangerous heat or
  cold.
- It needs the ship to have **power** to stay open. With no power it fails shut. It draws no power itself.
- If you left it set to open, it **opens again by itself** once the hazard is gone and power is back.

The door's panel says why a vent you set open is shut: a hazard nearby, or no power. Airlock doors have no vent.
