title: Other ships at work

Other ships in your node don't just sit there. Traders, miners and patrols get on with their work, using the same shuttles,
pods and inspectors you do.

## Traders

A trader turns up beside the station it came to trade with. The station's shuttle flies over by itself, docks at one of
the trader's airlocks for a while, then flies home. After a pause it goes again, for as long as the trader stays.

- The station's shuttle stays at home while you are trading. If it is out when you start a deal, it turns back.
- If a fight breaks out in the node, it heads home.

## Miners

A mining ship turns up beside the rocks of the node's asteroid field. Its mining pod flies out, cuts ore off the
nearest rocks, and comes home, where the crew carry the ore into storage. Then it goes out again.

The rocks it cuts are the same rocks you mine, so a busy miner leaves less ore for you.

## Patrols

A navy or corporate patrol in your node sometimes inspects a trader or miner there. Its inspectors cross over by shuttle
and search the ship the way they search yours (see [Contraband and inspections](../inspections/)). An inspection of
another ship asks nothing of you and doesn't stop you from jumping. If they seize cargo, the starmap's
**News and rumours** reports it.

The inspectors go home if a fight breaks out.

## How busy a node gets

Only two of these things happen at once in a node: a shuttle visit, a mining trip or an inspection. The rest wait their
turn.
