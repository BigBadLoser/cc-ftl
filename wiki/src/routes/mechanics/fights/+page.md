title: Fights between other ships
order: 8
---

Other ships have their own wars. Navy patrols hunt pirates, pirates go after traders, and rival corporations shoot at each
other. Most of this happens out of sight: the starmap's **News and rumours** reports who won. When it happens in the node
you are in, you watch it happen.

## When a fight breaks out near you

A fight starts in your node only when two fleets whose factions are at war end a turn there. Nothing is set up for you.
In a war zone or pirate country, where many hostile fleets pass through, you will see more fights.

- An urgent alert names the attacker and its target: **Join the fight? … attacks …**. Click it to jump to the ships.
- The two sides fight with their real weapons, shields and crews, just as you do. A ship that is losing badly charges its
  drive and jumps away.
- The fight ends when one side is destroyed, captured or gone. If neither side can finish the other after a few minutes,
  they break off. The ship's log and the news say how it ended.
- If you leave the node before the fight ends, it is settled without you.

You don't have to do anything. Staying out of it costs nothing.

## Joining a side

You join a fight by attacking one side. The first side you fire on (or board) makes you an ally of the other.

- **+2 standing** with the side you joined, and **+3 more** if your side wins. You get this once per fight, when it ends.
- Attacking a ship that wasn't hostile to you turns it and its fleet hostile, as it always does (see
  [Ship combat](../combat/)). Joining the pirates against a trader makes the trader's faction your enemy.
- If you fire on both sides, you get nothing.

To fight when neither side is hostile to you, call **battle stations** first. Your crew man the consoles and your weapons
charge. Then aim at the ship you want to hit.

## Allies come to your aid

Ships whose faction likes you come to help when you are attacked. If a hostile ship fires on you, armed ships in the node
whose faction you stand at **3 or more** with take it on, along with the ships it came with. This includes armed stations.

- They never help against their own faction or an ally of it.
- A ship that is already in a fight of its own keeps fighting that one.
- Helping you earns you nothing. Raise your standing by trading, doing jobs and joining their fights (see
  [Stations and trading](../trading/)).
