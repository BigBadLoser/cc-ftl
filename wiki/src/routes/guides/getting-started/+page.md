Build and maintain a spaceship colony. Direct crew, install modules, manage resources, explore the galaxy, and fight or negotiate with other ships.

## Your immediate priorities

1. **Keep essential systems functional.** A working power source and life-support equipment provide a foundation for survival.
2. **Check your supplies.** Construction consumes stored resources; a plan is only useful when the required materials are available.
3. **Assign work and monitor crew.** Crew members carry resources, construct equipment, operate systems, and respond to emergencies.
4. **Expand deliberately.** Every added module creates demands on space, power, and logistics.
5. **Prepare before traveling.** FTL travel requires charge and fuel. Check the route and the ship's capacity before jumping.

See [Power and reactors](../../mechanics/power/) and the [module reference](../../reference/modules/) for additional information.
